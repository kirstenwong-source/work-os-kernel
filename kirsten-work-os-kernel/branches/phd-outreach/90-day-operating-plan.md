# 90-Day Operating Plan For A PhD Outreach Role

## First 30 days: listen and map

- Meet internal stakeholders in recruiting, sourcing, research outreach, intern programs, and hiring teams.
- Build a map of priority universities, labs, research areas, faculty relationships, and existing engagement history.
- Review current metrics and define what counts as relationship quality, not just event attendance.
- Identify the top friction points in PhD engagement and handoffs.

## Days 31-60: design and pilot

- Select a small set of high-priority campuses or research clusters.
- Build engagement plans for each cluster.
- Create faculty meeting prep templates and post-event follow-up routines.
- Pilot a dashboard for prioritization and executive reporting.
- Translate internal research projects into academic-community narratives.

## Days 61-90: scale and improve

- Compare pilot outcomes against baseline engagement indicators.
- Recommend a repeatable campus engagement operating model.
- Establish a relationship review cadence with sourcing and research stakeholders.
- Create a quarterly insight brief on lab health, funding shifts, and emerging research clusters.
